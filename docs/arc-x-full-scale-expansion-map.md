# ARC X Full-Scale Expansion Map

Status: build map
Owner: ARK95X
Purpose: Expand the Environment Shaper system into every direction while keeping it usable, auditable, client-safe, and revenue-focused.

---

## 1. Prime Operating Loop

```text
Observe -> Decode -> Decide -> Act -> Capture -> Learn -> Productize -> Repeat
```

Every direction must plug into this loop or it is noise.

---

## 2. Non-Negotiable Guardrails

The system scales through trust, proof, and repeatable delivery.

```text
Allowed:
- research
- summarize
- compare
- draft
- extract
- organize
- score
- recommend
- prepare dashboards
- prepare outreach drafts
- prepare delivery drafts
- prepare client workflows
- prepare security and risk reviews

Commander approval required:
- sending
- posting
- contacting leads
- publishing
- changing accounts
- spending money
- taking payments
- sharing private information
- making legal, financial, medical, or reputational commitments

Never:
- fabricate proof
- guarantee income
- impersonate
- spam
- bypass platform rules
- misuse passwords or private data
- perform unauthorized investigations
- provide legal representation or legal strategy
```
